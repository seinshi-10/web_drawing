How to draw
Make a fist and point with only your index finger — that's the draw gesture.

Open your hand (or pinch thumb + index) to lift the pen without drawing.

Turn on your camera

AirDraw tracks your hand through your webcam so you can draw in the air with your fingertip. Nothing is recorded or uploaded — tracking runs entirely in your browser.

Give it a name — it'll be added to the public gallery.
